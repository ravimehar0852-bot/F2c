Frequently asked questions

Is submitting an enquiry the same as applying for a loan?

No. An enquiry lets our team understand your requirement and contact you. Approval depends on the lender and your profile.

What do I need to make an enquiry?

Your name, mobile number, email, the loan type and amount you need, your employment type and your city.

Will you ask for my bank password, PIN or OTP?

Never. Do not share these with anyone, including anyone claiming to represent us.

Are interest rates shown on this site?

No. Rates, fees and tenure vary by lender and applicant. The EMI calculator uses figures you enter, so its result is only an estimate.

Prefer to talk it through?

Call, message on WhatsApp or write to us. We will help you understand the options.
