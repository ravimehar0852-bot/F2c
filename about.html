How your application works

1. Create your account

Register with your name, mobile and email. You instantly get your Customer ID and Application ID.

2. Personal details

Enter your date of birth, 12-digit Aadhaar number, PAN, city, state and PIN code.

3. Employment & income

Tell us your occupation, employer or business and monthly income.

4. Loan requirement

Choose the loan type (personal, wedding, travel, medical, bill payment and more), amount and tenure. Your EMI is calculated automatically.

5. Documents

Your Aadhaar and PAN are filled in automatically. Add your bank name, account number and IFSC code.

6. Reference

Share the name and mobile number of one reference person.

7. Review & submit

Check all your details and submit. You receive an email with your IDs and a PDF summary of your application.

Important information

  • Interest rate: The indicative rate is 6% per annum. The final rate may vary based on credit history, existing financial obligations and the lender’s discretion.
  • Eligibility: Loan approval is subject to successful completion of KYC and income verification.
  • No guarantee: Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.
  • Safety: Never share your bank passwords, card PINs, UPI PINs or OTPs with anyone.
